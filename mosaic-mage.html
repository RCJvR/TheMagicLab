Mosaic Mage

Plan a flash: learners in a block, each one a pixel in one of two colours. Set the size of the block and the colours, click the grid to flip a learner between them, then save the picture under a name. Click through your saved pictures to run the show, and print a run sheet and a card for every learner.

Saved pictures

Drag a picture to change the order, or focus one and press Alt + ← or →. Present all, Next and printing follow this order.

Your group

Learners face the audience. Rows run across the picture and are lettered. Seats are numbered along each row.

Colours

Make a picture

Thicker lines are easier to read from far away. This changes text, shapes and photos as you drag it.

Words and logos read best with "Stretch to fill" and the line weight on Thin.

Shapes, text and photos are only a starting point. You can click any learner afterwards to fix it.

Animate

Create frames saves each step as its own saved picture, in order, so the cards and run sheet show every step.

Twinkle

Turn on Twinkle brush above the grid, then click or drag cells. They swap colours on their own (orange corner).

Timing

Print and share

Print uses your saved pictures that match the current grid size. Everything is kept in this browser automatically; Export all makes a copy you can move to another computer.
