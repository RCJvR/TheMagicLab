Admin Alchemist
Turn teacher paperwork into a few clicks: report comments in the CAPS achievement bands, marking rubrics, and question papers with a running mark total and a memo.
Everything you type stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored on our servers.
Write a report comment
Pick the learner's CAPS achievement level, tick what applies, and get a comment you can edit. Press Regenerate for different wording.
Class list
Comments you have added. Copy them all at once into your report system.
Build a rubric
Name the task, set the performance levels, then describe what each level looks like for each criterion. Total marks work themselves out.
Assemble a question paper
Add questions with their marks and cognitive level. The total checks itself against your target, and you can print the paper and a separate memo.
