QUESTION 4
4
Menstrual cycle (max 10)
FSH is secreted by the pituitary gland ✓
FSH stimulates the development of a follicle in the ovary ✓
The developing follicle secretes oestrogen ✓
Oestrogen causes the endometrium to thicken ✓
A high oestrogen level inhibits FSH secretion ✓
and stimulates the pituitary gland to secrete LH ✓
LH causes ovulation (about day 14) ✓
LH causes the empty follicle to develop into the corpus luteum ✓
The corpus luteum secretes progesterone ✓
Progesterone further thickens and maintains the endometrium, making it more glandular and vascular ✓
Progesterone inhibits FSH secretion, so no new follicles develop ✓
If there is no fertilisation the corpus luteum degenerates ✓
the progesterone level drops ✓
and the endometrium breaks down (menstruation) ✓
The low progesterone level allows FSH to be secreted again and a new cycle starts ✓

Water balance (max 7)
Sweating lowers the water content of the blood (the blood becomes more concentrated) ✓
This is detected by receptors in the hypothalamus ✓
The pituitary gland is stimulated to secrete more ADH ✓
ADH is carried in the blood to the kidneys ✓
ADH makes the walls of the collecting ducts (and distal convoluted tubules) more permeable to water ✓
More water is reabsorbed into the blood ✓
A small volume of concentrated urine is produced ✓
The water content of the blood returns to normal (negative feedback) ✓
