How readiness is worked out

For each skill a teacher rates their confidence from 1 to 4, then answers a short practical check.

  • The level starts at the confidence rating: Not yet Developing Ready Can lead
  • A wrong check answer caps the skill at Developing, and is flagged ! when the teacher felt confident.
  • A right answer with low confidence is flagged too: that teacher may need encouragement more than training.
  • Teachers who opted out (approved) are left out of every figure.
