Exam

  1. Type the code your teacher gives you.
  2. The paper opens in full screen. Do not leave it: switching to another tab, window or app is recorded, and your paper locks until your teacher unlocks it.
  3. Your answers save by themselves every few seconds. If the Wi-Fi drops, keep writing: they are kept on this computer and sent when it is back.
  4. The clock is the school's clock. When time is up, your paper is handed in as it is.
