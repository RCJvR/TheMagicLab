Number line.

Build a number line with your own range and increments — integers, decimals or fractions — and mark points, inequalities and intervals, or jumps for adding and subtracting.

Tips

  • Every value box accepts fractions and expressions: 3/4, -3/2 (for −1½), sqrt(2), pi.
  • For fractions: set the increment to e.g. 1/4 and choose Fractions or Mixed numbers.
  • Leave From or To empty for an inequality that carries on forever, e.g. x > 2.
  • Jumps: type the steps with spaces between, e.g. +5 -8 +2.
  • Choose Numbers: None for a worksheet where learners fill in the numbers themselves.
