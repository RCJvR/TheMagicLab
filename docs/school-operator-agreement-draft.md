# Operator agreement (POPIA sections 20–21): The Magic Lab and a school

**DRAFT, 2026-11-12. Not legal advice. Have it reviewed by someone qualified in POPIA before
any school signs it.** Square brackets mark the parts to fill in for each school.

This agreement is between **[School name]**, [address], represented by [name, title] ("the
School"), and **The Magic Lab**, represented by Ruhan Janse van Rensburg, Information Officer
("the Operator").

## 1. Why this agreement exists

The School's teachers and learners use The Magic Lab (themagiclab.co.za) for teaching,
practice and assessment. When they do, the Operator processes personal information on the
School's behalf. Under POPIA the School is the **responsible party** and The Magic Lab is its
**operator**. Section 21 of POPIA requires a written contract that obliges the operator to keep
the information secure and confidential. This is that contract.

Where a teacher or learner uses The Magic Lab privately, outside the School's arrangement,
The Magic Lab is the responsible party for that use under its published Privacy Policy, and this
agreement does not apply to it.

## 2. What information, about whom

| Data subjects | Personal information |
|---|---|
| Learners of the School (most are children) | Name as shown on the platform, email address, grade, class membership; lesson progress, quiz and test results; work handed in to teachers (text and images); teachers' marks, comments and annotations; optionally the admission number, if a teacher adds it |
| Teachers of the School | Name, email address, classes taught, marking activity |

No special personal information (POPIA section 26) and no ID numbers are processed.

## 3. Purpose

The Operator processes this information **only** to provide The Magic Lab's services to the
School: accounts and sign-in, lessons and practice, handing in work, marking and feedback, the
teacher's mark book and exports of it, and support. The Operator does not use it for
advertising, does not sell it, and does not use learners' work to train AI models.

## 4. The Operator's obligations

The Operator will:

1. Process the information only on the School's documented instructions (this agreement and
   the School's use of the platform's settings) unless the law requires otherwise, and tell the
   School if it believes an instruction breaks POPIA.
2. Keep the information confidential, and allow access only to people who need it to run the
   service and who are bound to confidentiality.
3. Maintain appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures (POPIA section 19),
   including at least: encryption in transit and at rest; row-level security so that a learner
   sees only their own information and a teacher only the classes they teach; private storage
   for learners' images with links that expire; handed-in work that cannot be altered; a log of
   every change to a mark; and least-privilege access for staff.
4. Not give platform administrators blanket access to learners' handed-in work. Access for
   support happens only when the School asks, is limited to what the request needs, and is
   recorded.
5. Use only the sub-operators listed in clause 6, and tell the School at least 30 days before
   adding or replacing one, so the School can object.
6. Help the School respond to requests from data subjects (access, correction, deletion,
   objection) within [10] working days of the School's request.
7. Notify the School **without undue delay, and within 72 hours** of becoming aware of
   reasonable grounds to believe the School's information has been accessed or acquired by an
   unauthorised person (POPIA section 22), with what is known about the incident and what is
   being done. The School decides whether and how to notify the Information Regulator and the
   people affected; the Operator will help.
8. Keep the records needed to show it meets this agreement, and answer the School's reasonable
   questions about its security once a year, or after an incident.

## 5. The School's obligations

The School will:

1. Have a lawful basis for the processing, including, for learners under 18, the consent of a
   competent person where POPIA section 35 requires it, or another ground that section allows.
2. Tell learners and parents that the School uses The Magic Lab, what it is used for, and where
   to find the Privacy Policy.
3. Ask teachers to add only the information the platform needs (for example, not to put
   personal details in comments that do not belong there).
4. Tell the Operator when a teacher leaves, so their access to the School's classes can end.

## 6. Sub-operators and where the information is stored

| Sub-operator | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase Inc. | Database, sign-in and file storage | AWS eu-west-1, Ireland (European Union) |
| GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.) | Serves the website's pages; holds no learner information | Global CDN |
| Anthropic PBC | Only if the School turns on AI features: generates AI Tutor replies and, if added later, AI marking suggestions that a teacher must confirm | United States |

Storing the information in the European Union is a transfer outside South Africa. It is made
under POPIA section 72: the recipient is bound by the EU General Data Protection Regulation and
by a data processing agreement with the Operator. If the School requires its information to
stay in South Africa, the parties will discuss whether and how that can be done; it is not
available today.

## 7. Retention, return and deletion

1. Handed-in work (text, images and annotations) is deleted automatically **18 months after the
   end of the academic year** it belongs to, unless the School asks in writing for a different
   period.
2. Marks in teachers' mark books are kept while the class exists, so the School has its
   assessment records.
3. When this agreement ends, the Operator will, at the School's choice, give the School an
   export of its classes' marks (spreadsheet) and learners' handed-in work (files), and then
   delete the School's information within [60] days, except what the law requires it to keep.
   The Operator will confirm the deletion in writing.
