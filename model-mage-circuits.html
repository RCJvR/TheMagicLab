How to build: drag a part from the toolbar onto the board. Click one pin, then click another to draw a wire — click empty board space in between to bend the cable and keep routing tidy (hold Ctrl to snap that bend to horizontal/vertical). Click a part or wire to edit it in the panel. Press Delete to remove the selected part or wire.
