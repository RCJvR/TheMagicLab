Bible Pictionary How to play

  1. Split into two teams. Each team gets its own topic phone.
  2. Each team chooses one person to draw. Take turns, so everyone gets a go.
  3. The drawer presses Pick a card and secretly chooses one item on it.
  4. Don't let your team members see the cards!
  5. Tell your team the category, then start drawing.
  6. No talking, acting, letters or numbers.
  7. Your team shouts out guesses. You have 60 seconds.
  8. A correct guess in time wins 1 point. Then swap drawers.
  9. The team with the most points wins!
