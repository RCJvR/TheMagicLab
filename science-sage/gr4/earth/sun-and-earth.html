The Sun and the Earth

Our star

The Sun is a giant ball of hot gas: our closest star. The Earth travels around it once a year, at just the right distance for life.

1 The Sun: our closest star

The Sun is a star. It is a giant ball of hot gas that gives out heat and light. It is very big and very far away, but it is the closest star to the Earth.

Hot gasThe Sun is not solid. It is made of very hot, glowing gas.
Very bigAbout 109 Earths could fit side by side across the Sun
Very farAbout 150 million kilometres away. Sunlight takes about 8 minutes to reach us.
Stay safeNever look straight at the Sun. It can damage your eyes.
Make a model

Big Sun, small Earth

Cut a circle 109 cm wide from paper for the Sun. Next to it, draw a dot 1 cm wide for the Earth. How many times bigger is the Sun?

2 Moving around the Sun

The Sun is at the centre of the solar system. The Earth is one of eight planets that move around the Sun. The path a planet follows is called its orbit.

The centreThe Sun is in the middle of the solar system
OrbitThe pathway of the Earth around the Sun
One yearThe Earth takes about 365 days to go around the Sun once
8 planetsMercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune
Act it out

Human solar system

One learner is the Sun in the middle of the field. Eight learners are the planets and walk slowly around the Sun in circles. Who takes longest to go around once?

3 The Sun and life

The Earth gets just the right amount of light and heat from the Sun for life. If it were closer, it would be too hot. If it were further away, it would be too cold.

Light for plantsPlants need sunlight to make food
HeatThe Sun keeps the Earth warm enough to live on
Liquid waterNot too hot and not too cold, so the water stays liquid
LifeWithout the Sun, there would be no life on Earth
Investigate

Near and far

Put two ice cubes in the sun: one close to a sunny window and one far from it. Which melts first? How is this like the planets?
