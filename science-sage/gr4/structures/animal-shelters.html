Structures for Animal Shelters

Build a safe home

Animals need shelter. Some build their own, and people build some for them. Then you design one!

1 Natural and human-made shelters

A shelter keeps an animal safe, dry and comfortable. Some shelters are natural. People make others: they are human-made.

NaturalBird nests, snail shells, hollow trees, wasp nests
Human-madeDog kennels, bird cages, kraals, stables
Why shelter?To stay out of rain, wind and sun, and safe from danger
Case study

Rex needs a home

Rex the puppy sleeps outside. Last night it rained and the wind was cold. In the day there is no shade. What problems does Rex have? What kind of shelter would help him?

2 Shell and frame structures

A shell structure is a hard, solid covering, like an egg. A frame structure is made of parts joined together, like the bars of a cage.

ShellSolid outside, hollow inside: egg, snail shell, tortoise shell
FrameBars or poles joined together: bird cage, kraal fence, tent
Shapes and sizesShelters come in different shapes and sizes, made from different materials
Picture sort

Shell or frame?

Look at pictures of animal shelters. Sort them into two groups: shell structures and frame structures. Which materials is each one made from?

3 Design an animal shelter

Engineers design, draw and then evaluate (check) their ideas. Think about the shelter's purpose, its shape and size and its materials.

PurposeWhich animal is it for? What must it protect it from?
Shape and sizeBig enough for the animal to stand, turn and lie down
MaterialsStrong and waterproof, like wood or metal sheets
EvaluateDoes it do its job? What could be better?
Design task

My shelter design

  1. Choose an animal that needs a shelter.
  2. Draw your design and label the materials.
  3. Write down its shape and size.
  4. Evaluate it: is it dry, strong, safe, the right size and airy?
