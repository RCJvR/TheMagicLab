Strong Frame Structures

Triangles are strong

Join struts into triangles and you get a strong, stable frame, like bridges, cranes, pylons and traditional homes.

1 Triangles make frames strong

A square frame wobbles and squashes flat. A triangle keeps its shape. Add a strut across a square and you get two strong triangles.

SquarePush the corner and it leans over
TriangleStays the same shape when you push it
Add a diagonalOne strut across a square makes it strong
Build it

Square or triangle?

Join four paper struts into a square with paper fasteners. Push one corner. What happens? Now join three struts into a triangle and push it. Add a fifth strut across your square. Is it stronger now?

2 Frames all around us

Struts joined into triangles make strong, stable structures, such as roof trusses, bridges, cranes and pylons. Even our skeleton is a frame: limb bones are struts.

Roof trussesTriangles of wood hold up the roof
Pylons and cranesTall steel frames full of triangles
SkeletonLeg and arm bones are strong struts
Triangle hunt

Spot the triangles

Look at pictures of bridges, cranes and pylons, or look around the school: a roof, a goal post, a climbing frame. Draw one structure and colour in every triangle you can find.

3 Traditional homes and your own structure

Traditional homes in South Africa are built on a framework of struts, such as branches and poles: the Zulu uguqa, Xhosa homes (rontabile and ungqu-phantsi) and the Nama matjieshuis.

Zulu uguqaBent saplings tied into a dome, covered with grass thatch
Xhosa homesA frame of poles and branches, with walls of mud and a thatched roof
Nama matjieshuisA dome of bent branches covered with reed mats; it can be packed up and moved
Design project

Design, make and evaluate a strong structure

  1. Design: choose a tower, a bridge, a pylon or a chair. Draw your design and show where the triangles go.
  2. Make: build it from rolled paper struts, sticky tape and paper fasteners.
  3. Evaluate: test it. Does it stand without wobbling? How much weight can it hold? How could you make it stronger?
