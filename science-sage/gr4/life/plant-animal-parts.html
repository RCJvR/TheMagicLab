Structure of Plants & Animals

Parts with jobs

Every part of a plant or animal has a job that helps it stay alive.

1 The parts of a plant

Most plants have roots, a stem, leaves, flowers and fruit with seeds. Each part has its own job.

RootsDrink water and hold the plant in the soil
StemHolds the plant up and carries water
LeavesMake food using sunlight
FlowerMakes the seeds
FruitHolds and protects the seeds
Eat your plants

Which part am I eating?

We eat different parts of plants! Sort these: carrot, celery, spinach, broccoli, apple, peas, potato, cauliflower.

Make a poster with six columns: root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit, seed.

2 No two plants look the same

All plants have the same kinds of parts, but they look different. Their parts can have a different size, shape and colour.

SizeA grass is knee-high. A thorn tree is taller than a house.
ShapeGrass leaves are long and thin. Sunflower leaves are big and wide.
ColourAloe flowers are orange-red. Protea flowers are pink.
Plant walk

Compare three plants

Find three different plants on the school grounds. Draw a table in your book with a row for each part: roots, stem, leaves, flowers, fruit, seeds. Write how each plant's parts look.

3 The parts of an animal

Animals have a head, body, limbs, a tail and sense organs. Animals look different: they have a different size, shape, body covering and sense organs.

LimbsLegs, wings or fins help animals move
CoveringFur, feathers or scales protect the body
MouthTeeth or a beak to fit the food
Sense organsEyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin find food and spot danger
