Living & Non-living Things

Alive or not?

How can we tell if something is living? Let's be life detectives!

1 The 7 things living things do

Something is living only if it does all 7 life processes. Remember them with the name MRS GREN.

M · MoveRun, swim, or turn to the sun
R · BreatheTake in air (respire)
S · SenseNotice things and react
G · GrowGet bigger over time
R · ReproduceMake babies or seeds
E · ExcreteGet rid of waste
N · FeedTake in food (nutrition)

A car moves and uses fuel, but it can't grow or have babies. So a car is not living.

Germs are living too! They are so small that we can't see them without a microscope. Bread mould is living as well.

Get moving

Act out MRS GREN

Your teacher calls a letter. Everyone does the action!

  • M: march on the spot
  • R: take a huge breath in and out
  • S: jump as if you heard a loud bang
  • G: crouch small, then stretch up tall
  • R: rock a pretend baby
  • E: wipe sweat off your forehead
  • N: munch a pretend apple

2 Living, never alive, or once alive?

Everything fits in one of 3 groups. Some things were living and are now dead, like dry leaves and dead wood.

LivingDoes all 7 life processes now
Never aliveRocks, rivers, fire, plastic
Once living, now deadDead wood, dry leaves, feathers, bones
Treasure hunt

Classroom hunt

With a partner, find 2 things from each group in the classroom or on the playground. Draw them in your book under three headings.

Bonus: find something that is tricky to sort, and tell the class why.

3 Things that seem dead, but are alive

Dry beans, dried yeast and a bird egg look like they are not living. Give them the right conditions and they carry on living!

Dry bean seedWater and warmth make it germinate (start to grow)
Dried yeastWarm water and sugar wake it up. It makes bubbles!
Fertilised eggKept warm, a chick grows inside and hatches
Try it for real

Grow a bean in a jar

  1. Stuff a clear jar with wet cotton wool.
  2. Push 3 dry beans against the glass.
  3. Make a second jar with dry cotton wool.
  4. Put both on a windowsill. Draw them on day 1, 4 and 7.
Class demo

Wake up the yeast

Put a spoon of dried yeast and a spoon of sugar in a bottle of warm water. Stretch a balloon over the top. Wait 20 minutes. What happens to the balloon? Why?
