Habitats of Animals

Home, wild home

Every animal lives in a place that gives it what it needs. Let's explore Africa's habitats!

1 What is a habitat?

A habitat is the place where a plant or animal lives. It gives the animal everything it needs to stay alive.

FoodPlants or other animals to eat
WaterTo drink, or to live in
ShelterA place to rest, out of the sun, wind and rain
BabiesA safe place to have and raise young
SafetyPlaces to hide and escape from danger
Habitat hunt

Find a habitat at school

Look under a rock, in a tree, in long grass or near a tap. Draw the habitat you find and the animals that live there. What does the habitat give them?

2 Different kinds of habitats

There are many kinds of habitats, like grassland, forest, river and sea. Each one is home to different animals.

GrasslandWide, open land covered with grass and a few trees
ForestLots of tall trees growing close together
RiverFresh water flowing over land, with muddy banks
SeaSalty water, very big and deep
Group poster

Habitat posters

Each group gets one habitat. Draw it and add at least four animals that live there. Next to each animal, write one reason why it can live there.

3 African animals fit their habitats

African wild animals have body parts that suit their habitat. That is why they can live there.

Giraffe · grasslandA long neck reaches leaves high up in thorn trees
Hippo · riverEyes and nose on top of its head, so it can hide in water and still see and breathe
Cape fur seal · seaThick fur and fat keep it warm in the cold sea
