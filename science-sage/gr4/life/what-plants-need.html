What Plants Need to Grow

Grow, plant, grow!

What does a plant need to grow big and healthy? Let's find out, and measure it.

1 What do plants need?

Plants need light, water and air to grow. Seeds need water and warmth to start growing.

LightLeaves use sunlight to make food
WaterNot too little, and not far too much
AirLeaves take in air to make food
WarmthSeeds need warmth to germinate

2 Measure how a plant grows

Scientists measure and record. Measure the stem with a ruler and count the leaves every few days.

MeasureHeight of the stem in centimetres (cm)

Fair testChange only one thing, like light or water
Class project

Bean race

  1. Plant two beans in the same kind of pot and soil.
  2. Put one on the windowsill and one in a dark cupboard.
  3. Give both the same water every 2 days.
  4. Every 2 days, measure both and count their leaves. Record it in a table: Day · Height (cm) · Leaves.

Which one grew taller? Which one looks healthier? Are those the same thing?

3 New plants from cuttings

New plants can grow from seeds, and also from cuttings: a piece of stem cut from a plant.

CutCut a piece of stem with a few leaves
WaterStand it in water or damp soil
Roots and leavesNew roots grow from the stem, then new leaves
Try it for real

Grow a cutting

Ask an adult to cut a piece of stem from a plant such as spekboom, mint or a geranium. Stand it in a jar of water on a windowsill. Look for roots every 2 days and draw what you see.
