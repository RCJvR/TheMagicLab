Sound

Can you hear that?

Every sound starts with a vibration. Find out how sound travels to your ears, how to make loud, soft, high and low sounds, and how to protect your hearing.

1 Vibrations make sound

All sounds are made by vibrations. Sound moves outwards in all directions from the part that vibrates. It travels through air, water and solids like wood and metal, all the way to your ears.

Feel itTouch your throat while you hum. You can feel the vibrations!
It travelsThrough air, water and solids. It is fastest in solids.
Your earOuter ear → ear canal → eardrum → tiny bones → inner ear
Your brainA nerve carries the message to your brain. Now you hear!
Investigate

Dancing rice

  1. Stretch plastic wrap tightly over a bowl and put a few grains of rice on top.
  2. Bang a pot with a spoon close to the bowl.
  3. What happens to the rice? Why?

2 Loud and soft, high and low

We can make sounds loud or soft. This is the volume. We can also make sounds high or low. This is the pitch.

LoudBig vibrations: hit the drum hard
SoftSmall vibrations: tap it gently
High pitchFast vibrations: a bird, a whistle, a short string
Low pitchSlow vibrations: a lion's roar, a big drum, a long string
Try it

Voice and bottles

Sing one note softly, then loudly. Now sing a high note and a low note. Then fill bottles with different amounts of water and tap them. Which bottle gives the highest note?

3 Noise pollution

Sound that is loud, unpleasant or harmful and goes on for a long time is called noise pollution. It can damage your hearing forever.

At homeLoud music, TV, lawnmowers, headphones turned up high
In the communityTraffic, taxis, building sites, factories, vuvuzelas
Protect your earsMove away, turn it down, cover your ears, wear earmuffs or earplugs
Hearing aidsHelp people who are hearing-impaired to hear better
Survey

Noise detectives

List the noisy places at home, at school and in your community. Which are the worst? Make a poster that tells people how to protect their ears.
