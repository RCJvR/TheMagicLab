Energy Around Us

Energy everywhere

Movement, heat, light and sound are all energy. Find where energy is stored, and how machines use it.

1 Energy all around us

We can notice energy all around us. It shows up as movement, heat, light and sound.

MovementA rolling ball, a running dog, the wind
HeatA fire, the sun on your skin, a hot stove
LightThe sun, a light bulb, a torch
SoundA bell, music, someone talking
Energy walk

Find the energy

Walk around the school. Find two examples each of movement, heat, light and sound energy. Draw them in a table with four columns.

2 Stored energy

Energy can be stored in sources such as food, wood, coal, oil products and natural gas. We move the energy from the source to where we need it.

FoodStores energy for our bodies
Wood and coalBurn them to get heat and light
Oil productsPetrol, diesel, paraffin, jet fuel and candle wax are all made from crude oil
Natural gasBurns in gas stoves and heaters
At home

Energy stores at home

Ask your family which energy sources you use at home for cooking, heating, light and transport. Make a list and share it with the class.

3 Input and output energy

Machines and appliances need an input of energy to make them work. They give us an output of energy that is useful to us.

InputThe energy that goes in: electricity, petrol, or movement from your body
MachineChanges the energy into a useful kind
OutputThe useful energy that comes out: heat, light, sound or movement
