Equations of motion for uniform acceleration — 1D and vertical projectiles.

v = u + at
Displacement
s = ut + ½at²
Velocity–displacement
v² = u² + 2as
Average velocity
v̄ = (u + v) / 2

a = −g = −9.8 m·s⁻²
