How ready do you feel to teach each part of CAT?

Rate your confidence on each skill, and answer the short check question where there is one. You get your own readiness profile straight away, with the Computer Codex lessons that cover anything you want to brush up on. It takes about 15 minutes, and you can stop and carry on later.

Who sees this? You, and the CAT curriculum lead assigned to your school. It is used to plan training and support, not to appraise performance. If you have a good reason not to take part, you can request to opt out; your lead approves it.
